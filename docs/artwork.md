# Bundled Artwork

## Desktop Wallpaper

Asset: `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/desktop_wallpaper.webp`.

Original artwork generated for MagicDesk with the built-in Codex image generation
tool. The delivered image is 1672 x 941 pixels, encoded as WebP at quality 90,
method 6. It contains no text or logo and supports centered landscape,
ultrawide, and portrait crops. The app decodes it without resource-density
scaling, then prepares a display-sized pixel frame.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original bundled desktop wallpaper for
> MagicDesk, an Android desktop environment. Generate a finished high-resolution
> raster wallpaper, landscape 3840x2160 if supported. Quiet sophisticated abstract
> broad folded surfaces with soft studio illumination and subtle translucency;
> restrained graphite and neutral silver with a small amount of cyan reflected
> light matching MagicDesk's UI. Low visual clutter, soft material transitions,
> enough tonal contrast to read white desktop icon labels without harsh luminous
> areas. The design must work as a center crop on a 21:9 ultrawide monitor and as a
> narrow centered portrait crop on a phone, so the central region should be
> balanced and interesting, with no important isolated object at the edges.
> Edge-to-edge artwork only. No text, no logo, no watermark, no screenshot, no UI,
> no device frame. No stars, no glowing orbs, no bokeh, no tiny repetitive pattern.
> Do not imitate any phone manufacturer's wallpaper. Deliver one original polished
> wallpaper.
